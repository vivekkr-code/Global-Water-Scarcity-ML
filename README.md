# Global Water Scarcity & Stress Prediction

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing global freshwater availability, water consumption, and water stress across different countries using Data Science and Machine Learning.

The objective is to identify patterns in freshwater usage, compare water stress between countries, and develop predictive models to understand water resource challenges.

This project is aligned with **United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 6 (SDG 6): Clean Water and Sanitation**.

## Objectives
- Analyze global water stress levels.
- Study freshwater withdrawals across countries.
- Identify countries facing higher water pressure.
- Explore historical water consumption trends.
- Create meaningful data visualizations.
- Develop Machine Learning models for water stress prediction.

## Datasets
The project uses the following datasets:

1. **Global Water Stress Dataset** – Measures freshwater withdrawals as a percentage of available freshwater resources.
2. **Freshwater Withdrawals Dataset** – Provides information about freshwater extraction across countries.

### Dataset Sources
- [Global Water Stress – Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/freshwater-withdrawals-as-a-share-of-internal-resources)
- [Annual Freshwater Withdrawals – Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/annual-freshwater-withdrawals)
